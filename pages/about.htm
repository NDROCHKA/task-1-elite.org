About us

Built around coffee.
Made for people.

A fictional neighbourhood roastery created to demonstrate a complete multi-page HTML and CSS website.

Our approach

Specialty coffee without the complicated language.

Ember & Oak began as an idea for this student project: a focused coffee brand with a warm identity, a clear product collection, and a welcoming physical space.

Every fictional coffee has an origin, roast level, and short flavour description. The goal is to make choosing a bag feel simple even if someone is completely new to coffee.

Our visual direction follows the structure of a modern retail website while changing the brand, subject, imagery, and writing into something original.

What matters

Three simple principles.

01

Choose carefully

Start with coffees that have clear origins, thoughtful processing, and plenty of natural character.

02

Roast clearly

Use roast levels people understand and describe flavour without making the experience intimidating.

03

Share generously

Create a room, a product, and a website that make people feel welcome from the beginning.

The room matters too

A place designed for unhurried coffee.

Warm timber, natural light, and a small menu keep the focus on good cups and good conversations.
