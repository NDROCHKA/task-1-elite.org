Our story

A small roastery with a wide-open door.

Ember & Oak began as a fictional idea for a student project: make specialty coffee feel clear, warm, and easy to enjoy.

What we believe

Specialty coffee does not need to feel complicated.

It can be as simple as knowing where a bean came from, when it was roasted, and what you might taste in the cup. We use plain language because good coffee should invite people in.

Our fictional roast list is intentionally short. Light coffees stay bright, darker coffees keep their sweetness, and every bag includes a straightforward brewing suggestion.

From source to cup

Three careful steps.

01

Choose with purpose

We imagine working with transparent importers and producers who care about quality and long-term relationships.

02

Roast with restraint

Each roast is designed to reveal the coffee’s natural sweetness instead of covering it with unnecessary bitterness.

03

Share while fresh

Coffee is packed quickly, labelled clearly, and served with practical guidance for brewing it at home.

The room matters too

Designed for conversations that take their time.

The roastery combines warm timber, natural light, and an open coffee bar. There is no rush: stay for one espresso or spend the afternoon with a book.
