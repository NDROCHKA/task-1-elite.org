About us

Built around coffee.
Made for people.

A neighbourhood roastery built around fresh coffee, clear flavours, and a welcoming everyday experience.

Our approach

Specialty coffee without the complicated language.

Ember & Oak began with a focused idea: create a warm coffee brand with a clear collection and a physical space people genuinely enjoy visiting.

Every coffee has a stated origin, roast level, and short flavour description. The goal is to make choosing a bag feel simple, even for someone completely new to coffee.

We roast in small batches, share practical brewing guidance, and keep the experience welcoming from the first question to the final cup.

What matters

Three simple principles.

01

Choose carefully

Start with coffees that have clear origins, thoughtful processing, and plenty of natural character.

02

Roast clearly

Use roast levels people understand and describe flavour without making the experience intimidating.

03

Share generously

Create a room, a product, and a website that make people feel welcome from the beginning.

The room matters too

A place designed for unhurried coffee.

Warm timber, natural light, and a small menu keep the focus on good cups and good conversations.
